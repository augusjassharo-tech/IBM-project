FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

Q1: What is the minimum attendance required to write exams?
A1: A minimum of 75% attendance is required. Students between 65-74% can apply for
condonation with a medical certificate.

Q2: How are internal marks calculated?
A2: Internal marks (50 total) are based on two internal tests, assignments, and
attendance, and are added to the 50 marks from the end-semester exam.

Q3: What should I do if I lose my ID card?
A3: Report immediately to the college office to get a duplicate ID card issued. A small
fee may apply.

Q4: When does the library open and close?
A4: The library is open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on all working days, Monday to Saturday.

Q5: How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?
A5: Submit a request form at the administrative office with your roll number and
reason for the certificate. It is usually issued within 3 working days.

Q6: Who do I contact for project guide allocation?
A6: Contact your Head of Department (HOD) or the final year project coordinator listed
on the department notice board.

Q7: What is the procedure for revaluation of answer scripts?
A7: Submit a revaluation application along with the prescribed fee within 10 days of
result publication, through the examination cell.

Q8: Are mobile phones allowed in the examination hall?
A8: No, mobile phones and other electronic devices are strictly prohibited in
examination halls.

Q9: How can I get a transcript or provisional certificate after graduation?
A9: Apply through the examination cell with your final semester marksheets and a
processing fee. Processing typically takes 7-10 working days.

Q10: Where can I find placement and internship updates?
A10: Placement and internship notices are posted on the official college notice board
and shared through the placement cell's official communication channels.
