COLLEGE ACADEMIC REGULATIONS

1. Attendance Policy
Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject during a semester
to be eligible to write the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between
65% and 74% may apply for condonation with a valid medical certificate. Students below
65% attendance will not be permitted to appear for the examination and must repeat the
semester.

2. Examination Rules
Students must carry their college ID card to every examination. Use of mobile phones or
any electronic device inside the examination hall is strictly prohibited and will be
treated as a case of malpractice. Each theory exam is of 3 hours duration, and answer
scripts must be submitted before the invigilator announces the end of the exam.

3. Internal Assessment
Internal assessment marks are calculated based on two internal tests (15 marks each),
assignments (10 marks), and attendance (10 marks), totaling 50 internal marks. The
remaining 50 marks are allotted to the end-semester university examination.

4. Grading System
The college follows a 10-point Grade Point Average (GPA) system. A minimum of 5 GPA is
required to pass a subject. Students who score below the passing grade must reappear for
the arrear examination in the subsequent semester.

5. Project Work (Final Year)
Final year students must complete a project as part of their curriculum. The project
includes a project proposal review, two progress reviews, and a final viva-voce
examination. Project reports must be submitted in the format prescribed by the
department at least one week before the final review.

6. Code of Conduct
Students are expected to maintain discipline on campus. Ragging in any form is strictly
prohibited and punishable under the law. Any student found involved in ragging will face
immediate suspension and may be expelled from the college.
